## Books

Two books are described in this section. Characters can find [The Codicil of White](99%20Game%20Mechanics/CLI/items/the-codicil-of-white-idrotf.md) in Auril's abode (see chapter 5) and [The Incantations of Iriolarthas](99%20Game%20Mechanics/CLI/items/the-incantations-of-iriolarthas-idrotf.md) in the Netherese necropolis hidden under the Reghed Glacier (see chapter 7).

## Wizard Spells

Characters who obtain [The Codicil of White](99%20Game%20Mechanics/CLI/items/the-codicil-of-white-idrotf.md) from Auril's abode or [The Incantations of Iriolarthas](99%20Game%20Mechanics/CLI/items/the-incantations-of-iriolarthas-idrotf.md) in Ythryn can discover one or more of the following wizard spells.

- [Blade of Disaster](99%20Game%20Mechanics/CLI/spells/blade-of-disaster-tce.md)  
- [Create Magen](99%20Game%20Mechanics/CLI/spells/create-magen-idrotf.md)
