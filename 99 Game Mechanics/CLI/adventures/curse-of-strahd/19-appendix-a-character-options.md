# Appendix A: Character Options
*Source: Curse of Strahd, p. 209* 

## Character Background

During character creation, players can select the following background for their characters, with your approval: [Haunted One](99%20Game%20Mechanics/CLI/backgrounds/haunted-one-vrgr.md). It is appropriate for any character or campaign associated with eeriness or horror.
