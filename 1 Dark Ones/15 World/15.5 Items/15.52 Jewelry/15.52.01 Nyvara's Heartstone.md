# Nyvara’s Heartstone
*Wondrous item, uncommon*

#### Features

- **Guardian's Favor:** Once per short rest, you can call upon the heartstone's magic to gain advantage on any one skill check you make. Additionally, when you do so, you can choose to regain 1d4 hit points.

#### Description

The Heartstone is a smooth, palm-sized gemstone that shifts from deep crimson to a warm golden hue in the light. It emits a soft, rhythmic pulse, hand-like warm to the touch, and has faint ethereal wisps curling off it, suggesting its magical nature.

#### Lore

A token from her father gifted to Nyvara before he was sent to prison for theft, this heartstone is said to contain a fragment of his undying spirit, offering protection and guidance in her time of need.
