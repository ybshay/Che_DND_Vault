# Quasit Wand
*Wand, uncommon*
#### Features
- **Quasit Soul:** Contains the soul of a [[quasit]] that can be summoned with [[find-familiar|Find Familiar]]
- +2 to all Spell Attacks
- +2 to Spell Save DC
#### Lore
- Given by Azazel as a trade for [[Ice Gray Staff]]
- The soul from the [[Ice Gray Staff]] jumped into the quasit’s body when it was first summoned, and now controls its body
