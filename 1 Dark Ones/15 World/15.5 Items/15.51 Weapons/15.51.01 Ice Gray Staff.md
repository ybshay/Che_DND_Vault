# Ice Gray Staff

#### Features

- Converts all spells cast to **Ice**
- +2 to all spell attack and damage rolls
- Requires attunement and usage as arcane focus
- Makes the holder’s nails long and unkempt

#### Lore

- Looted off the hag who set up the orc camp at Wyvern-Tor
- Speaks in the mind of the person holding it
- Every person who attuned to this item became known to it as Grizzella
- Its favourite owner was Grizzella
- As is the norm with hag magic, this item may slowly consume its user’s soul on behalf of its original keeper
- Owned by a hag, then looted by [[Nivara Keris]], who traded it to Azazel for [[Quasit Wand|Quasit Wand]]
