# [[11.1 Nyvara Keris|Nyvara Keris]] Lore

---
#### <u>Part 0: Tidbits & Ideas</u>
Nyvara and her sister each look really similar to one of their parents. Nyvara looks like the father, and Sara looks like the mother.
#### <u>Part 1: Family History & Early Life</u>
Her father, a thief from Phandalin who moved to Neverwinter, was young Nyvara's role model. Nyvara idolises her father, and largely interprets his flaws as being strengths.

Nyvara and and her sister were with their mother in Nyvara’s early years, living in Neverwinter’s Blacklake district. When their mother passed, they moved down to the docks with their father.
#### <u>Part 2: Adolescence</u>
Her father was eventually captured and sent to prison, leaving Nyvara and her sister Sara with their great-aunt, Reyna Dreamfire, and her grandson Damon. Reyna, already teaching Damon about magic, did the same for Nyvara.
#### <u>Part 3: Adult Life (Pre-Campaign)</u>
After several years of competition, Damon eventually went too far and injured Nyvara (but was stopped by her sister). Afterwards, both sisters were sent back to Phandalin for their own safety.
