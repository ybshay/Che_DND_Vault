# Nyvara Emotional Progression 1

---
> [[Unique Notes|Unique Notes]], [[11.1 Lore|Lore]]

Because she’s gotten away, she’s gotten more confident. Gotten real friendships. She’s happier and freer than she used to be, and getting stronger. Her false confidence is starting to turn into real confidence. She’s learning new, stronger magic than what she was born with and taught how to use. She’s just started learning it, hasn’t learned the full details of her deal yet. 

Then she thinks she’s killed her mentor & captor. A weight’s been lifted off her shoulders, but she’s deeply affected by the loss of that big figure in her life. That event was also started by her using this new magic that felt unfamiliar but empowering to use. 

She’ll soon discover that her attempt to kill her grandmother had inadvertently resulted in bringing her back to life. That was not the choice she made, and she certainly doesn’t want her around given the shit she’s done/is going to do. She won’t ever defect to her grandmother, and realises the mistake she made. She also knows the grandmother might think she’s changed sides, and the party could still use that to stop her plans. That said, she doesn’t think she can go deep undercover because if she goes in alone, she might wind up getting manipulated herself. 

She’s also got to process that her grandmother was always a warlock, literally specialising in the magic that Nyvara was learning in order to remove her from power. Not only power in the world, but power over Nyvara herself. She’s disgusted with that type of magic right now, so she’s gone back to working on her sorcery instead.

That being said, Graz’zt is still lurking in her mind. She’s comparing this new patron to her experience with Azazel, her father, and the vision of her future that she used to have as a child. But she’s going back to sorcery so she can calm down and be less obsessed with proving herself. She knows she can eventually become a strong sorcerer, like her mother was, without losing her humanity like her grandmother did. 

She doesn’t necessarily want her grandmother dead, but she wants to stop her from doing any more harm, and she knows that her grandmother will probably stop at nothing for another chance to try and take over the world. As well as that, she owes her party everything because being with them has given her more confidence and relative peace than she’s ever really had before. Being around good people also helps her to keep herself accountable, to avoid repeating the cycles of trauma within her family- her grandma’s lust for power, her father thieving and scamming and generally just being an ass “for the family”. 

She’s in the process of realising Reyna can’t be saved, but she hopes that Damon still can be. Reyna’s favoritism put a wedge between them, but he used to be her best friend. Deep down, he’s not any more fucked up than she herself can be.
