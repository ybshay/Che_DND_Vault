#### description
Her magic feels, in a word, uncanny. She casts abnormally quickly or from much too far away, sometimes landing hits in places fate normally wouldn’t allow. From her black book of demon hymns, she slings spells that are typically impossible for a caster of her sort. She can power through at moments she should’ve been knocked out, and ease her own wounds by ending another’s life. Phantom hounds hunt her prey while she stalks between shadows, calling on her unholy benefactor to protect their investment. If the benefactor doesn’t deliver, her wings can always make for a speedy getaway. 

Even without the cover of shadow, she aids her allies’ cause with her other inherited skill- swaying people’s opinions in a more convenient direction. By assuming another’s identity or simply ordering someone to stay still, she can direct watchful eyes to look the other way. Through a self-importance one can only get from a lifetime of fake-it-til-you-make-it, she can convince suspicious individuals to welcome the party as guests in places they’re clearly not supposed to be.
