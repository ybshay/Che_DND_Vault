> [[X Meta/Unique Notes|Unique Notes]], [[D02-1-Session Notes|Session Notes]]

We have two weeks to get to Icewind Dale, going through Neverwinter.

Reyna is one of the three generals under the Spider. The Spider will use the artefact and staff to raise the Sunken island of drow. 

We will be going to Axeholm to gather info before we head to Neverwinter. As it’s currently afternoon, we’ll be heading to Agatha’s to rest. 

We don’t get to Agatha’s. We find ourselves in a forest where the trees are too tall to see past. Vaelin sends his Echo up, but they cannot see past the top tree. 

We thought we were following a road, but we’ve lost our way. Nyvara senses much magic here, twisted magic. Tally senses that the area’s desecrated. Vaelin senses that nature’s forgotten what it is. 

The sun is red, the trees thin with sparse leaves. We reach the road & see two statue-like dogs sat still on the sides of the road. The Echo cannot see them. 

Tally can see tiny red eyes in the shade. 

We advance toward the statues and see only a river behind us, and a house nearby us. We meet a satyr inside who tells us there’s a city to the northeast. 

We learn from him the following:
- Neverwinter is 30 days away by boat.
- We’re located in an empire that’s been at war with the Sword Coast for 15 years. The year is 4004, our time is 143 years after the death of the now-very-alive-and-scarier-than-we-thought Etheril. 
- The drow protect this empire, and the land was once connected to the mainland. 
- In our timeline, Etheril’s death was heretical, and he was 9000 years old at the time and not at war.

Azura summons her dad, and we fill him in. He said something in Eldritch and went inside Azula’s necklace that his future self gifted to her. 

On the road, we see a poster ad for the Eldrane army, which can be joined at the facility in Krakow. We feel a shadowy presence as the house is surrounded by shadow. 

Every shadow we pass on the way to Krakow feels alive. We meet two guards at the entry, who let us pass without worry. They have similar drow armour to what we’ve seen before. Their skin looks drained and they have black scleras. They let us pass without worry. 

As we enter a tavern, we see every species inside is also drained. There’s a job board inside written in Elvish, and one of the jobs is to find the Spider (a sorcerer). 

Azura again summons her father, and we fill him in further. We learn that we’ve gone 400 years back in time. 

The area we’re sitting in is cursed, and a shadow of its former self. Azura’s father was planning to sail here from Neverwinter to find out the name of the god that died to cause our timeline to be referred to as ED. 

There’s a crack in the planar barriers and something is seeping through. It may be the Negative plane, but hopefully not because that shit’s insane. The people in this town are shadows, not alive, and with time, we will become like them. Azura’s father, ancient as he is, can feel the shadow energy. Azura’s mother is from the Fey realm. She’s an Eldrane elf, which is why she took to Azura’s father’s power so well and is so powerful. Powerful enough that alongside Ilimater, Reeve and Evelyn, she’s banned from entering Icewind Dale. 

Azura’s father Raphael believes the house is our key to escape. The owner didn’t summon us, though. He will grant us each a wish. Azura wished to remove the limtations of her fire abilities. Tally wished for loophole eternal youth. Vaelin wished to upgrade his sword to a legendary sword. Nyvara wished for the ability to fly - she can sprout wings made out of pure fire (with a speed of her walking speed). 

The general who will attack Phandalin and Xylen burst into the tavern and asked, ‘where are the miners?’. He wants them to get back to work digging the shadow shards. He didn’t pay his tab :(. He was a minotaur. 

We enter our rooms at the inn, cleaned by the tooth fairies- they have roughly the same red eyes Tally saw. As we go to our beds, we all feel a crunch. Tally peels back the sheets and sees teeth. Nyvara leaves 2 gold on her bed before she leaves. 

As we leave the inn, the entire town looks water-logged. The two guards at the entry are now dead with vines wrapped around them. Their skin looks more drowned than decayed. This town may have been part of the sunken city.

While we go down the road, the tiny red eyes are watching us again. Their voices beckon us to join them, and get louder as we proceed. When we come close to the house, we see two shadowy figures like the ones from before, now standing at the gate. 

Raphael had been summoned by Azura’s mother’s blood hitting her amulet.
