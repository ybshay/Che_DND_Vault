# Initial Ideas for Nyvara Warlock Pact with Graz’zt

---
> [[Unique Note|Unique Note]], [[13 Session Notes|Session Notes]], [[11.1 Lore|Lore]]

how/when the deal began: 
nyvara's mother, while pregnant with nyvara, demolished one of graz'zt's warlocks in a fight. this warlock was a political hitman. graz'zt tried to enter a deal with nyvara's mum, she refused. he saw she was pregnant and made a note to keep an eye on the family. 

when nyvara was speaking to Azazel, one of graz'zt's spies happened to be around and told graz'zt that someone he was keeping tabs on was gonna make a deal with Azazel. he waited for a good chance to reach out to nyvara and make a counter-offer. 

during the drow torture, graz'zt calls out to nyvara's mind and offers help with reyna in exchange for recruits. he says he can give nyvara an appetizer of abilities now, in exchange for the drow soul her familiar's currently killing, and they can work out a more specific deal later.

what nyvara wants from the deal:
- power / magic 
- influence / status
- cunning / guile

make her as good at these things as Reyna and Damon; better, even

quietly, harmlessly manoeuvre reyna out of court and install a replacement of the phandalin guild’s choosing (because nyvara isn’t interested in either of her or graz’zt being further involved)

- or that she may try to get him working for graz'zt's "independent company" "something of a startup in the entertainment sector"
