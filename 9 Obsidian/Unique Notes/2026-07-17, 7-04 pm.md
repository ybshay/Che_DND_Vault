# 17th July 2026, 07:04:01 pm

---
> [[X Meta/Unique Notes|Unique Notes]]

Nyvara hears through Rufus’ sending stone that Tally, Ilimatar, Reeve and Phyllis are going after Reyna through a portal.

She follows Rufus and Leslie down to the temple entrance, where they’ve just seen Evelyn tell Azura to go fuck shit up.

The pair bursts through the door and fireball-nukes the entrance room, us following as they head to the next room. Inside the room is Gary and some other henchmen. Conflict ensues, and Gary just has a tragic time of things. Evelyn gets knifed and uno-reverses it before it lands, Hammun gets knifed and whimpers like a grandpa whose family wishes he’d retire. 

After that fight, Nyvara and co. join Tally and head to a room with Reyna and her goons in it. Reyna tries to monologue, but Azura interrupts with a fireball. Nyvara aims for Reyna and lands a twinned Eldritch Blast, doing the most significant damage she’s ever done to an enemy. 

Rufus kills Reyna with an insane uppercut, and two of her guards begin to drag her body into another room as the rest of the party focuses on Reyna’s Death Knight. Once he’s killed, he knocks all party members save for Rufus and Nyvara. Nyvara, teleporting behind the two henchmen dragging Reyna’s corpse, deftly avoids being pushed back into a chasm and, thinking the chasm means certain death, uses [[gust-of-wind|Gust of Wind]] to push the henchmen into it. She soon realises this chasm was most likely the magic-buffing item Reyna had been looking for in Phandalin, and will absolutely revive her and her goons. 

All remaining members of the party are helped up by the guild members we came into the building with.
